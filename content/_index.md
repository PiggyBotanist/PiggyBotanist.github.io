Welcome! I'm **Jeremy (Kai-Hsiang) Chang**, a PhD student in Computer Science at McGill University, supervised by Jerome Waldispühl.

## Research Interests

My research focuses on:

- **Human-in-the-Loop AI**: Using large-scale video games for training AI models, particularly for antimicrobial resistance detection and disease biomarker identification
- **High-Dimensional Clustering**: Developing selective behavior cloning models with DQ-Networks, GNNs, and LLMs to enhance metagenomics binning
- **Machine Learning Applications**: Applying ML pipelines to proteomics and metabolomics data for predictive modeling
- **Computational Biology**: Metagenomics, biomarker identification, and optimization of biological processes

## Current Work

I'm currently conducting research on high-dimensional clustering using large-scale video games for Human-in-the-Loop AI training, aimed at antimicrobial resistance detection and disease biomarker identification. I'm developing selective behavior cloning models with DQ-Networks, GNNs, and LLMs in PyTorch to enhance metagenomics binning.
