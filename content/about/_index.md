## About Me

I am a PhD student in Computer Science at McGill University, supervised by Jérôme Waldispühl. My research focuses on developing DNA embeddings that capture species-specific signals for metagenomic clustering, leveraging reinforcement learning techniques such as behavior cloning and contrastive learning with annotations derived from large-scale video games.

## Research Interests

- **Game With Purpose**: [Borderland Science](https://borderlands.2k.com/news/borderlands-science/)
- **Behaviour Cloning**
- **LLMs for Biological Sequences**
- **Multiomics Data**
