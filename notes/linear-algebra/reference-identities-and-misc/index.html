Reference: Key Identities and Miscellaneous

This page collects useful formulas and concepts that appear across linear algebra and numerical analysis.

Condition number#

For invertible $A$ and a chosen norm:

$$ \kappa(A) = |A| ,|A^{-1}|. $$

In 2‑norm, $\kappa(A) = \sigma_{\max}/\sigma_{\min}$, the ratio of largest to smallest singular value. Large $\kappa(A)$ means the system $A\mathbf{x}=\mathbf{b}$ is ill‑conditioned (small input errors can cause large output errors).

Matrix norms#

  • Induced norms: $|A|p = \sup{\mathbf{x}\ne0} \frac{|A\mathbf{x}|_p}{|\mathbf{x}|_p}$.
  • Frobenius norm: $|A|F = \sqrt{\sum{i,j} |a_{ij}|^2} = \sqrt{\text{tr}(A^T A)}$.

Block determinants and Schur complement#

If $A$ is invertible,

$$ \det \begin{pmatrix} A & B \\ C & D \end{pmatrix} = \det(A), \det(D - C A^{-1} B), $$

where $D - C A^{-1} B$ is the Schur complement of $A$.

Useful identities#

  • $\text{tr}(AB) = \text{tr}(BA)$.
  • $\det(e^A) = e^{\text{tr}(A)}$.
  • $(I + AB)^{-1}A = A(I + BA)^{-1}$ (when inverses exist).
  • Sherman–Morrison: $$ (A + \mathbf{u}\mathbf{v}^T)^{-1} = A^{-1} - \frac{A^{-1}\mathbf{u}\mathbf{v}^T A^{-1}}{1 + \mathbf{v}^T A^{-1}\mathbf{u}}. $$

Quadratic forms and definiteness#

For symmetric $A$, $f(\mathbf{x}) = \mathbf{x}^T A \mathbf{x}$:

  • $A \succ 0$ (PD) iff $f(\mathbf{x})>0$ for all $\mathbf{x}\ne0$.
  • $A \succeq 0$ (PSD) iff $f(\mathbf{x})\ge0$ for all $\mathbf{x}$.

Via spectral decomposition $A = Q\Lambda Q^T$:

$$ f(\mathbf{x}) = \sum_i \lambda_i y_i^2, \quad \mathbf{y} = Q^T \mathbf{x}. $$

Rayleigh quotient#

For symmetric $A$ and $\mathbf{x}\ne0$,

$$ R(\mathbf{x}) = \frac{\mathbf{x}^T A \mathbf{x}}{\mathbf{x}^T \mathbf{x}} $$

lies between the smallest and largest eigenvalues. Maximizing (or minimizing) $R(\mathbf{x})$ gives the principal (or minimal) eigenvector.
