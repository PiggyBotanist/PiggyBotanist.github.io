Gearbox Collaboration - McGill University

February 22, 2026 · 47 words · Jeremy (Kai-Hsiang) Chang

Design engaging minigame similar to Borderland Science (BLS) encoded with biologically relevant data for high quality annotation and Contrastive Learning. Direct collaboration with Gearbox Softwares for prototyping of the minigame using Unity, Unreal Engine, and ReactJS (Three.js) for UI and AWS, FastAPI, and PostgresSQL as a backend.
