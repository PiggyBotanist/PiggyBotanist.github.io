Dot Product

02. Products and Geometry

Inner products measure alignment and angles; cross products in $\mathbb{R}^3$ produce perpendicular vectors and areas/volumes. Dot product (inner product) For $\mathbf{u}, \mathbf{v} \in \mathbb{R}^n$: $$ \mathbf{u} \cdot \mathbf{v} = \mathbf{u}^T \mathbf{v} = \sum_{i=1}^n u_i v_i = |\mathbf{u}|,|\mathbf{v}| \cos\theta. $$ Key facts: $|\mathbf{u} \cdot \mathbf{v}| \le |\mathbf{u}|,|\mathbf{v}|$ (Cauchy–Schwarz). $\mathbf{u} \cdot \mathbf{v} = 0 \iff$ $\mathbf{u}$ and $\mathbf{v}$ are orthogonal. Cross product ($\mathbb{R}^3$) For $\mathbf{u}, \mathbf{v} \in \mathbb{R}^3$: $$ \mathbf{u} \times \mathbf{v} = \det \begin{bmatrix} \mathbf{i} & \mathbf{j} & \mathbf{k} \\ u_1 & u_2 & u_3 \\ v_1 & v_2 & v_3 \end{bmatrix} = \begin{pmatrix} u_2 v_3 - u_3 v_2 \\ u_3 v_1 - u_1 v_3 \\ u_1 v_2 - u_2 v_1 \end{pmatrix}. $$ ...
