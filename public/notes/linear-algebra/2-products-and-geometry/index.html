02. Products and Geometry

January 1, 2024 · 1 min · 169 words · Your Name
Table of Contents
  • Dot product (inner product)
  • Cross product ($\mathbb{R}^3$)

Inner products measure alignment and angles; cross products in $\mathbb{R}^3$ produce perpendicular vectors and areas/volumes.

Dot product (inner product)#

For $\mathbf{u}, \mathbf{v} \in \mathbb{R}^n$:

$$ \mathbf{u} \cdot \mathbf{v} = \mathbf{u}^T \mathbf{v} = \sum_{i=1}^n u_i v_i = |\mathbf{u}|,|\mathbf{v}| \cos\theta. $$

Key facts:

  • $|\mathbf{u} \cdot \mathbf{v}| \le |\mathbf{u}|,|\mathbf{v}|$ (Cauchy–Schwarz).
  • $\mathbf{u} \cdot \mathbf{v} = 0 \iff$ $\mathbf{u}$ and $\mathbf{v}$ are orthogonal.

Cross product ($\mathbb{R}^3$)#

For $\mathbf{u}, \mathbf{v} \in \mathbb{R}^3$:

$$ \mathbf{u} \times \mathbf{v} = \det \begin{bmatrix} \mathbf{i} & \mathbf{j} & \mathbf{k} \\ u_1 & u_2 & u_3 \\ v_1 & v_2 & v_3 \end{bmatrix} = \begin{pmatrix} u_2 v_3 - u_3 v_2 \\ u_3 v_1 - u_1 v_3 \\ u_1 v_2 - u_2 v_1 \end{pmatrix}. $$

Properties:

  • $|\mathbf{u} \times \mathbf{v}| = |\mathbf{u}|,|\mathbf{v}| \sin\theta$ (area of parallelogram).
  • $\mathbf{u} \times \mathbf{v} = -(\mathbf{v} \times \mathbf{u})$ (anticommutative).
  • $\mathbf{u} \times \mathbf{v} = \mathbf{0} \iff \mathbf{u}$ and $\mathbf{v}$ are parallel.

Triple products:

  • Scalar triple: $\mathbf{u} \cdot (\mathbf{v} \times \mathbf{w}) = \det[\mathbf{u},\mathbf{v},\mathbf{w}]$ (signed volume).
  • Vector triple: $\mathbf{u} \times (\mathbf{v} \times \mathbf{w}) = \mathbf{v}(\mathbf{u} \cdot \mathbf{w}) - \mathbf{w}(\mathbf{u} \cdot \mathbf{v})$.
