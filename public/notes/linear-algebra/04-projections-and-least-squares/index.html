04. Projections and Least Squares

January 1, 2024 · 1 min · 200 words · Your Name
Table of Contents
  • Projection onto a line
  • Projection onto a subspace
  • Least squares

Projection finds the closest point in a subspace; least squares uses this idea to solve inconsistent systems by minimizing error.

Projection onto a line#

Given nonzero $\mathbf{a}$ and any $\mathbf{b}$, the projection of $\mathbf{b}$ onto $\text{span}{\mathbf{a}}$ is

$$ \text{proj}_{\mathbf{a}}(\mathbf{b}) = \left( \frac{\mathbf{a} \cdot \mathbf{b}}{\mathbf{a} \cdot \mathbf{a}} \right) \mathbf{a} = \frac{\mathbf{a}^T \mathbf{b}}{|\mathbf{a}|^2},\mathbf{a}. $$

The perpendicular component is

$$ \mathbf{b}^\perp = \mathbf{b} - \text{proj}_{\mathbf{a}}(\mathbf{b}), $$

and satisfies $\mathbf{b}^\perp \cdot \mathbf{a} = 0$.

Projection onto a subspace#

Let $W = \text{Col}(A)$, where $A$ has linearly independent columns. The projection of $\mathbf{b}$ onto $W$ is

$$ \text{proj}_W(\mathbf{b}) = P\mathbf{b}, \qquad P = A(A^T A)^{-1} A^T. $$

Here $P$ is the projection matrix onto $W$, with $P^2 = P$ and $P^T = P$.

Least squares#

If $A\mathbf{x} = \mathbf{b}$ has no exact solution ($\mathbf{b} \notin \text{Col}(A)$), we instead minimize

$$ E(\mathbf{x}) = |\mathbf{b} - A\mathbf{x}|^2. $$

The minimizer $\hat{\mathbf{x}}$ solves the normal equations

$$ A^T A \hat{\mathbf{x}} = A^T \mathbf{b}. $$

Then $\hat{\mathbf{x}} = (A^T A)^{-1} A^T \mathbf{b}$ when $A$ has full column rank. The residual

$$ \mathbf{e} = \mathbf{b} - A\hat{\mathbf{x}} $$

is orthogonal to the column space: $\mathbf{e} \perp \text{Col}(A)$. This is the basis of ordinary least squares regression and many fitting problems.\n
