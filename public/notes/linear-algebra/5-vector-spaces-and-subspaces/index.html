5. Vector Spaces and Subspaces

January 1, 2024 · 1 min · 148 words · Your Name
Table of Contents
  • Vector space axioms (over $\mathbb{R}$)
  • Subspace test

Vector spaces capture the algebra of addition and scalar multiplication; subspaces are “closed” subsets behaving like smaller vector spaces.

Vector space axioms (over $\mathbb{R}$)#

For $V$ with vectors $\mathbf{u},\mathbf{v},\mathbf{w}$ and scalars $c,d$:

  1. $V$ is closed under $+$ and scalar multiplication.
  2. Addition is commutative and associative.
  3. There is a zero vector $\mathbf{0}$ and additive inverses $-\mathbf{v}$.
  4. Scalar multiplication distributes: $c(\mathbf{u}+\mathbf{v}) = c\mathbf{u}+c\mathbf{v}$ and $(c+d)\mathbf{v} = c\mathbf{v}+d\mathbf{v}$.
  5. Scalar multiplication is associative: $c(d\mathbf{v}) = (cd)\mathbf{v}$.
  6. $1\mathbf{v} = \mathbf{v}$.

Examples include $\mathbb{R}^n$, spaces of matrices, polynomials, and many function spaces.

Subspace test#

A subset $H \subseteq V$ is a subspace iff:

  1. $\mathbf{0} \in H$.
  2. If $\mathbf{u},\mathbf{v} \in H$, then $\mathbf{u}+\mathbf{v} \in H$.
  3. If $\mathbf{u} \in H$ and $c \in \mathbb{R}$, then $c\mathbf{u} \in H$.

If these hold, all vector space axioms are inherited from $V$.\n

Common subspaces: $\text{Col}(A)$, $\text{Row}(A)$, $\text{Nul}(A)$, any span of vectors, and spaces of polynomials of degree $\le n$.
