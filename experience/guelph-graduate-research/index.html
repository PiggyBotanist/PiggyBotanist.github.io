Graduate Research Student - University of Guelph

January 1, 2024 · 117 words · Jeremy (Kai-Hsiang) Chang

Overview#

During my Master’s program at the University of Guelph, I applied machine learning techniques to analyze high-dimensional biological data for predictive modeling and process optimization.

Research Projects#

Predictive Modeling for Fertility Status#

  • Applied and assessed standard ML pipelines including random forests, SVM, and classic neural networks on proteomics and metabolomics of urine samples
  • Developed models to predict litter size and fertility status of pigs
  • Worked with high-dimensional biological datasets

Feed Fermentation Optimization#

  • Optimized feed fermentation process by analyzing high-dimensional time-series data on temperature, moisture content, and pH level
  • Developed models to reduce anti-nutritional factors
  • Applied time-series analysis and optimization techniques
